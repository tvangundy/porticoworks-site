We produce experiences, not just ship code.

Computers can shrink the distance between people—or scatter attention into someone else’s cloud. We take the first path. Our work treats software as craft: clear interfaces, quiet infrastructure, and systems that keep family data, tools, and shared work close to the people who own them.

Think less “vendor roadmap,” more production house—story, materials, and release discipline— for the next way of living with machines.
