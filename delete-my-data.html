Delete my data

Use this page to ask Portico Works to delete personal data we hold about you for the Portico mobile app and related cloud services. Last updated: 15 August 2026.

How to request data deletion

  1. Email hello@porticoworks.dev from the address we should associate with the request (ideally your sign-in email).
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Portico data.
  3. Say whether you want to keep your account or also close it. To close the account entirely, prefer delete-me.porticoworks.dev.
  4. Describe any specific data you want removed if the request is limited (for example support tickets).

Data we can delete from our systems

  • Account profile details we store (such as email) when you also close the account, or fields you ask us to clear when policy allows.
  • Invite, mesh, and tunnel registration records tied to you on our cloud service.
  • Support correspondence and identifiers in operational logs, within retention limits.

We do not store your home lists, messages, or LAN app content in our cloud. That data stays on the host’s hardware (Portico Edge / home hub). Contact the person who invited you if you need that copy removed.

Data on your phone or tablet

  • Uninstall the Portico app to remove local app storage.
  • Disconnect the tunnel in the app and revoke VPN permission in system settings.
  • Private WireGuard keys are generated on device; uninstalling removes them from the app’s storage.

When deletion happens

We aim to fulfill verified data-deletion requests within 30 days. Backup systems may take a short additional period to roll forward. We will email you when the request is complete or if we need to verify identity.

Delete the whole account

To close your Portico account and remove account-scoped cloud data in one request, use delete-me.porticoworks.dev.
