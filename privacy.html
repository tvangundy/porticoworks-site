Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy covers Portico: the mobile app (Android package com.porticoworks.mobile), accounts and invitations, Cloud Agents we host, Edge Agents you run, and related services at porticoworks.dev.

Who we are

Portico Works, LLC (“Portico Works,” “we,” “us”) operates Portico. Contact: hello@porticoworks.dev.

What Portico does

Portico is a private place to share lists, conversations, checklists, reminders, and files with people you choose. An agent holds that material. A Cloud Agent is one we host for you. An Edge Agent is the same Portico on a computer you keep, at home or at work.

An invitation is the permission. It names the agent and the specific things that person may open. Accepting it does not open the rest of the agent. Adding someone as a friend only puts them on a people list; friendship does not grant access.

People join with the Portico app or a browser. Away from the local network, the app can set up a private encrypted connection to that agent and open only what the invitation includes. Portico is not a general-purpose VPN.

Where information lives

  • Account service — sign-in, billing status, which agents you own, invitations and the things they grant, your friends list, and a lightweight catalog (names and how to route to a list, not the items, messages, or files inside it).
  • Cloud Agent — if you use one, the live lists, conversations, reminders, and related app data are on infrastructure we operate. That is convenient to start. It is not the same as keeping the disk yourself.
  • Edge Agent — live lists, conversations, reminders, files, and AI settings stay on hardware you control. We do not have that disk. Portico Files folders are stored here, not on a Cloud Agent.
  • Your phone or tablet — the app keeps your tunnel private key on the device. The account service receives the public key and does not keep the invitee’s private key after setup.

On a Cloud Agent, that agent’s own connection keys are on the hosted agent we operate, because we are running it. On an Edge Agent, those keys stay on your computer.

Information we collect

  • Account — email, display name, and authentication details. You can sign in with email or with Google, Apple, Microsoft, or GitHub where those options are offered. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Sponsored accounts — if you create a sign-in for someone else, we store the username and display name you set, and a birth date when the account is for a child. You set the password. A test account may also have an expiration date.
  • Invitations and access — who invited whom, which agent, which lists, folders, apps, or devices the invitation includes, and connection status needed to provide access.
  • Friends — names and email addresses you add to your roster.
  • Device and tunnel — a WireGuard public key, tunnel endpoints, and diagnostics you or a host send for support.
  • Notifications — if you allow alerts, a push token for that device so the agent or our notification service can deliver them.
  • Operations — logs of errors and connectivity so we can keep the service reliable. We do not sell personal information.

Portico AI

If the person who runs the agent turns on Portico AI, chat transcripts and memories you confirm stay on that agent. The model provider the host configures receives the prompt for that request, including any memory text included in it. A provider on the public internet sees those prompts. A model running on the local network does not send them there. The host’s API key stays on the agent. Invitees can use the feature without receiving the key. Nothing is saved as memory until a person confirms it.

Backup

Agents can store encrypted backup archives with us. Encryption happens on the agent before upload. A backup you create with your own passphrase is ciphertext we cannot read; we do not have that passphrase. Automatic backups on a Cloud Agent use a key we manage so they can run without you typing a passphrase. Those snapshots are still encrypted, and they are not protected by a secret only you know. Restore runs on the agent.

How we use information

  • Create and authenticate accounts, including sponsored accounts.
  • Record invitations and enforce what each one allows.
  • Run Cloud Agents you ask us to host, and coordinate Edge Agents you run.
  • Provide the private connection, including relay when someone connects from outside the local network. Relay carries encrypted traffic. It is not where lists, messages, or files are stored.
  • Deliver notifications you allow, take payments, operate backups, and secure and improve the service.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to run Portico:

  • With people on an invitation, limited to what that invitation includes. The person who runs the agent can see who they invited and, on that agent, what those people add.
  • With a sponsor, for a sponsored account: sign-in security, friend approvals, invitations, and supervision stage. Sponsoring an account does not give the sponsor the lists, messages, or files on that person’s agent.
  • With infrastructure providers that process data for us, including Stripe for payments and a push-notification provider for alerts.
  • With the model provider the host configures, when Portico AI is on, limited to the prompt for that request.
  • When required by law.

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context advertising.

Network / VPN permission

On Android, Portico uses the system VPN capability so traffic for an invitation can use an encrypted tunnel. The OS shows a VPN indicator while the tunnel is on. You must consent to the system VPN prompt. Tunnel traffic is encrypted between your device and the configured endpoint. You can disconnect in the app or revoke VPN permission in system settings.

Retention

We keep account, invitation, and agent records while they remain active, and for a reasonable period afterward for security and legal obligations. Deleting a Cloud Agent removes the live data on that hosted agent, subject to encrypted backup retention on your plan. Live data on an Edge Agent or a home cluster stays on that hardware; we cannot delete it from here. Ask the person who runs that agent if you need your copy removed.

To delete your account, visit delete-me.porticoworks.dev. To request deletion of personal data we hold, visit delete-my-data.porticoworks.dev, or email hello@porticoworks.dev.

Your choices

You can decline an invitation, leave one you accepted, disconnect the tunnel, turn off notifications, or uninstall the app. The person who runs an agent chooses who is invited and what each invitation includes. You can ask us to delete account data we hold.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Portico after an update means you accept the revised policy.
