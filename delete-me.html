Account

Delete my account

Use this page to request deletion of your Portico account (Android app package com.porticoworks.mobile and related Portico Works cloud services). Last updated: 15 August 2026.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email hello@porticoworks.dev from the same address you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Portico account.
  3. Include your account email and, if you know it, the invite or household name you used.

We will confirm the request and delete the account. You do not need to keep the app installed to complete this process.

What we delete

  • Your Portico / console account record (email and authentication details we hold).
  • Invite and access records tied to your account on our cloud service.
  • Tunnel registration data we store for your account (for example public keys registered with the service).
  • Operational logs that identify your account, within our normal log retention window.

What we may retain

  • Records we must keep for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons (kept only as long as required).
  • Aggregated, non-identifying statistics.
  • Data that lives only on a host’s home hardware (lists, apps, or files on their Portico Edge / home hub). We do not host that content in our cloud; ask the household host if you need that data removed from their systems.

When deletion happens

We aim to complete account deletion within 30 days of a verified request. You will receive an email when it is done (or if we need more information to verify ownership).

On your device

After deletion (or anytime), uninstall the Portico app and revoke VPN permission in system settings if it remains. That removes local app data and any on-device tunnel private key.

Delete data without closing the account

If you only want certain data removed but want to keep signing in, see delete-my-data.porticoworks.dev.
